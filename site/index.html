Lay your notes out like a map.

%name% is an infinite canvas for your notes. Place them as cards, gather them into zones, draw the links between them, and zoom out to see how it all fits. Every note stays a Markdown file in a folder you own.

Download Try it in your browser

Cards, zones and links

A card is a note

Double-click the paper to place a card and start typing. The first line is its title; the rest is Markdown.

Zones group them

Ctrl drag on the paper to draw a zone. Zones nest, and moving one brings everything inside along.

Links connect them

Ctrl drag from one card to another. Give the link a label, a dashed or dotted line, arrowheads at either end.

Tab keeps you going

Press Tab on a card to add a linked card after it, already open for typing.

Many cards at once

Type a few lines, get a map

Press Ctrl B, write one idea per line and join them with arrows. The preview lays them out as you type.

Pilot study -> Main study --> Write-up
Pilot study .-> Ethics approval
Main study <=> EEG lab

One dash is a line, two are dashed, a dot is dotted, = is double.

Rewire links as text

Open the builder on cards already on the canvas: their links come back as lines to edit. Arrange lays the cards out along them.

Zoom out to see the whole map

Zones become regions

Below 40%, each zone shows the outline of what it holds, loose cards gather into counted stacks, and the links between two areas merge into one line with a count.

Quick with thousands of cards

The canvas only loads what is on screen. Click a stack, or double-click a zone, and it glides in until the cards show.

Find your way back

Search as you type

Ctrl F searches every note and zone name. Click a result and the canvas glides there.

Every zone in a list

Ctrl L lists the zones as a tree, with a dot on the ones in view and how many cards each holds.

Nothing gets lost

Notes without a card wait in the Unplaced tray until you drag them onto the canvas.

Write by hand

A pen on the canvas

Press P and draw: circle a card, underline a line, sketch what words can't. A stylus draws thinner or thicker with pressure.

Drawings stay put

Strokes group into drawings that belong to their zone and move with it. One eraser takes whole strokes, the other cuts through them.

Plain files you own

A vault is a folder

Notes are Markdown files named after their titles, readable in any editor. The canvas sits beside them as small JSON files, one per card, zone, link and drawing.

Sync it your way

Keep the folder in Syncthing, Dropbox or Google Drive. Two devices only clash when they change the same item, and a note changed elsewhere is never overwritten: your text is saved beside it.

No account, no server

Nothing leaves your computer unless you sync it. Fonts and all are inside the app.

Five themes

Studio: a cool gray canvas dotted at the grid crossings, white cards and a petrol accent.

Engineering pad: sage graph paper, index cards with a red rule under the title, fountain-pen blue.

Orbit: a deep navy grid with brighter nodes where its lines cross, and cards edged in light.

Cairn: peat-moss paper, slate cards with a dim gold rule, fog-pale text.

Outer Wilds: deep-space indigo with stars at the grid crossings, warm cream text and a campfire-orange accent.
